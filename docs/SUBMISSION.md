## Suggested title

PATCH IRL — Small fixes. Real life.

## Short description

Turn a photo of everyday physical friction into one achievable improvement. PATCH IRL uses Muse Spark to plan around your real constraints, Muse Image to visualize the proposed change, and a second real photo to check visible progress. Export a reality patch and take the plan into the world.

Edit the description if Muse Image was not successfully exercised; describe its integration as implemented but unavailable in the tested account or run, and explain the annotated fallback.

## Theme and originality

Theme: Build for Your World. Intended track: indie developer/student.

Original work created November 4 during the event: constraint-driven intervention prompts, validated Meta inference routes, approximate photo grounding, before/concept comparison, action tracking, reality-patch PNG/JSON exports, actual-after assessment and the bilingual user experience. Reused infrastructure: Sites Vinext starter, React, Lucide icons, Zod and third-party dependencies. Models are hosted by Meta.

## Why it is more than a simple analyzer

The project should demonstrate an actual constrained intervention, a portable action artifact and a real-world follow-up. A description of a photo alone would not fulfill the concept, and the event guide explicitly lists simple image analyzers among anti-projects. Eligibility and scoring remain the organizers' decision.

## Required final checks

- [ ] Spark succeeds on your real BEFORE photo.
- [ ] Read and review the generated plan before applying it.
- [ ] Muse Image succeeds, OR show the real failure and label the annotated fallback honestly.
- [ ] Actual AFTER is a real photo; compare it against the plan.
- [ ] PNG and JSON downloads work and contain the correct labels.
- [ ] README validation record matches observed behavior.
- [ ] Public repository URL works while signed out; no secrets or private photos in its history.
- [ ] Three-minute video has been recorded, reviewed, uploaded and is viewable without requesting access.
- [ ] Live product link is accessible to judges if submitted; do not confuse an owner-private preview with public access.
- [ ] All actual contributors are listed accurately. Disclose AI coding assistance where the form asks.
- [ ] Submit through your accepted participant account on the CV platform.
- [ ] Observe a confirmation screen or receipt; save it.

Do not invent users, measured outcomes, benchmarks, model calls or completed physical changes. A working demo supports a claim of potential; it does not establish broad real-world impact.
